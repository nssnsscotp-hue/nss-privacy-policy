Account Deletion Request

App: NSS Units 36 & 94 NSSCOTP

Developer: TATVANYX

If you wish to permanently delete your account and associated personal data from the NSS Units 36 & 94 NSSCOTP application, you may request deletion using the method below.

How to Request Account Deletion

Send an email to:

nssnsscotp@gmail.com

Use the following subject:

Account Deletion Request

Please include the following information:

  • Your Full Name
  • Your Registered Username
  • Your Registered Email Address
  • Reason for deletion (optional)

What Happens After Your Request

After verifying your request, we will process your account deletion within 7 working days.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Volunteer account information
  • Username
  • Email address
  • Blood bank information (if applicable)
  • Uploaded files and photos associated with your account, where applicable

Data That May Be Retained

Certain records may be retained when necessary for legitimate administrative purposes, including:

  • Attendance records
  • Participation records
  • Program reports
  • Information required by law or institutional policy

Retained information is kept only for legitimate administrative purposes and is not used for marketing.
