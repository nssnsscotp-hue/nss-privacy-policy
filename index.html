Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 19 July 2026

TATVANYX operates the NSS Units 36 & 94 NSSCOTP mobile application.

Information We Collect

The app may collect:

  • Name
  • Username
  • Email address
  • Password
  • Class/Department
  • Blood donor information
  • Photos uploaded by volunteers

Email

Email addresses are used only for account management and password recovery using Email OTP.

Blood Bank

The app may collect Name, Blood Group, Phone Number and Place/Class/Department for voluntary blood donation activities.

Location

The app requests location access only when the user activates the Emergency SOS feature. The app does not continuously track location.

Camera

Camera permission is used only for QR code attendance scanning.

Notifications

The app may send notifications regarding NSS programmes and announcements.

Public & Volunteer Access

Public users can access limited features without registration. Volunteer-only features require registration and administrator approval.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user information.
